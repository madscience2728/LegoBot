2-wheel-drive rebuild (single hub). Speed is -1.0..1.0. The right wheel gets its speed negated on the phone (WheelMap.kt) before sending, so positive speed on either wheel now means the same physical direction -- confirmed by hand after the rebuild: port C (left) needed no correction, port A (right) did, the head-tilt servo (port B) needed none either. Tilt "center/forward" preset is -7°, not 0 -- the mount's weight tilts it forward a bit at rest, so true level isn't the servo's zero point. Up (-45) and down (+45) are unchanged, full-extent targets.
